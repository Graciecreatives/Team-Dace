Speaker notes Product scope & honest limits

01 · One farm. Everything connected.

FarmBuddy brings planning, operations and business records into a connected workspace. This is a product overview, not a claim that every legacy tool or every external service automatically synchronizes. Artwork is original illustration, not a real farm or screenshot. Source: docs/CONNECTED_MANAGEMENT.md and app/components/FarmWorkspace.vue.

02 · Less chasing. More farming.

The contrast describes a workflow benefit, not measured time savings. Notes, team tasks, inventory and business records exist in the app. No productivity or adoption metrics are claimed. Source: FarmWorkspace.vue, FarmOperations.vue and FarmBusiness.vue.

03 · Four simple ways in.

These are editorial groupings of existing capabilities, not a literal reproduction of application navigation. Grow/care includes production planning and event history. Jobs includes assignments and stock usage. Sales and bookkeeping remain distinct actions. Source: FarmProduction.vue, FarmOperations.vue, FarmBusiness.vue.

04 · Plan the season.

Seasons support crops or livestock, optional plot/register links, schedules and dated events such as feeding, health, breeding, production and observation. Recording an animal health event is not veterinary advice or automated diagnosis. The field outline and all dates/tasks implied by this mockup are illustrative. Source: docs/CONNECTED_MANAGEMENT_CONTRACT.md and server/utils/farm-production.js.

05 · Give every job a clear owner.

Owners/managers manage tasks. Workers can complete assigned tasks and record permitted inventory usage. Team invitations require explicit acceptance, not automatic email delivery. Example people and tasks are fictitious. Do not imply payroll, worker tracking or generalized access to owner settings. Source: FarmOperations.vue and server/utils/farm-operations.js.

06 · One harvest, connected flow.

A harvest adds a harvest event, stock movement and inventory quantity together. Creating an order draft does not reduce stock or record income. Explicit confirmation of a paid cash sale atomically reduces stock and creates one income record. This does not collect a payment. Confirmed-order reversal is not implemented. Storefront stock mapping must be explicit, not assumed. Source: docs/CONNECTED_MANAGEMENT_CONTRACT.md and server/utils/farm-business.js.

07 · Your farm becomes your shop.

The storefront supports identity, hero copy, cover photos, themes and published product listings. Publication and public contact consent are explicit. Buyers contact the farm directly. There is no online checkout, payment processing or automatic stock reservation. The farm slug is a site path, not a custom domain. Source: app/components/FarmMarketplace.vue and app/pages/[farmSlug]/products.vue.

08 · A clearer area view.

Road tiles use OpenStreetMap, dated satellite context uses NASA GIBS, and forecasts require configured OpenWeather access. These are opt-in online services. Satellite context is regional, approximately 250 m, can be cloudy or delayed, and is not NDVI, live field monitoring, yield prediction or diagnosis. Moving the map does not change the forecast location. Source: docs/FARM_WATCH_MAP.md and FarmSatelliteMap.vue.

09 · Point. Scan. Compare.

The Pl@ntNet specialist scanner returns candidate taxonomy and credited reference images. Any disease-screening suggestions are not validated diagnoses. Reference photos retain their provider credits/licences in the application. This presentation uses original vector artwork, not provider images or live results. Scanner access depends on provider configuration and connectivity. Source: docs/PLANT_SCAN_ACCEPTANCE.md and docs/CHAT_PHOTO_ACCEPTANCE.md.

10 · Ask in your own words.

Text chat uses Cloudflare Workers AI. The native text model is not a general photo model: photos go through separate specialist paths and supported results can be handed to chat. Dictation produces editable text and is never sent automatically. Optional farm context uses a server-selected snapshot of the farmer’s own records and curated references. Retrieval is not automatic training or fine-tuning, and chat has no record-writing tools. Do not promise external-provider zero retention. Spoken replies require separate YarnGPT configuration and are not claimed here. Source: docs/AI_FARM_CONTEXT.md, docs/WORKERS_AI_ACCEPTANCE.md and FarmAssistant.vue.

11 · Built for real farm work.

Offline workspace drafts are limited to event creation, task completion, task stock consumption and harvest creation. They are device/browser storage, not a full offline installation or background sync guarantee. Reconnect and explicitly replay; conflicts/errors require review. Account/farm scoping and permission checks apply on replay. D1 and R2 are Cloudflare deployment storage, not a promise of backup, uptime or certification. Source: app/utils/offline-workspace.js, useFarmWorkspace.js and docs/CLOUDFLARE.md.

12 · Start with one job.

This is a practical invitation, not a free-trial, pricing or availability claim. Start by planning a season, assigning a task, recording a harvest or checking the cashbook. No invented user counts, revenue, TAM or accuracy figures appear in this deck.
