Speaker notes Product scope & honest limits

01 · Smart AI Farming Management System

FarmBuddy helps with farm plans, jobs, stock and sales. It also offers AI farming advice and photo scans for plant identification and possible crop disease matches. Plant recognition uses a separate specialist service, not the AI text chat model. Scan suggestions are not confirmed diagnoses. Not every outside tool or service syncs automatically. Artwork is original illustration, not a real farm or screenshot. Source: docs/CONNECTED_MANAGEMENT.md and app/components/FarmWorkspace.vue.

02 · Your farm tools. One management system.

The contrast describes a workflow benefit, not measured time savings. Notes, team tasks, inventory and business records exist in the app. No productivity or adoption metrics are claimed. Source: FarmWorkspace.vue, FarmOperations.vue and FarmBusiness.vue.

03 · Four simple ways in.

These are editorial groupings of existing capabilities, not a literal reproduction of application navigation. Grow/care includes production planning and event history. Jobs includes assignments and stock usage. Sales and bookkeeping remain distinct actions. Source: FarmProduction.vue, FarmOperations.vue, FarmBusiness.vue.

04 · Plan the season.

Seasons support crops or livestock, optional plot/register links, schedules and dated events such as feeding, health, breeding, production and observation. Recording an animal health event is not veterinary advice or automated diagnosis. The application screenshot shows a sanitized demo workspace, not a live farm. Source: docs/CONNECTED_MANAGEMENT_CONTRACT.md and server/utils/farm-production.js.

05 · Give every job a clear owner.

Owners/managers manage tasks. Workers can complete assigned tasks and record permitted inventory usage. Team invitations require explicit acceptance, not automatic email delivery. Example people and tasks are fictitious. Do not imply payroll, worker tracking or generalized access to owner settings. Source: FarmOperations.vue and server/utils/farm-operations.js.

06 · Track your harvest from stock to sale.

A harvest adds a harvest event, stock movement and inventory quantity together. Creating an order draft does not reduce stock or record income. Explicit confirmation of a paid cash sale atomically reduces stock and creates one income record. This does not collect a payment. Confirmed-order reversal is not implemented. Storefront stock mapping must be explicit, not assumed. Source: docs/CONNECTED_MANAGEMENT_CONTRACT.md and server/utils/farm-business.js.

07 · Your farm becomes your shop.

The storefront supports identity, hero copy, cover photos, themes and published product listings. Publication and public contact consent are explicit. Buyers contact the farm directly. There is no online checkout, payment processing or automatic stock reservation. The farm slug is a site path, not a custom domain. Source: app/components/FarmMarketplace.vue and app/pages/[farmSlug]/products.vue.

08 · A clearer area view.

Road maps use OpenStreetMap, dated satellite pictures use NASA GIBS, and forecasts require configured OpenWeather access. These online services are optional. Satellite pictures cover a broad area, at approximately 250 m detail, and can be cloudy or delayed. They do not provide NDVI, live field monitoring, yield prediction or diagnosis. Moving the map does not change the forecast location. Source: docs/FARM_WATCH_MAP.md and FarmSatelliteMap.vue.

09 · Check your plants. Spot possible disease.

The specialist plant scanner uses Pl@ntNet to suggest plant names and show credited comparison photos. Plant recognition is separate from AI text chat. Crop disease checks offer possible matches, not confirmed or validated diagnoses. Reference photos retain their provider credits/licences in the application. This presentation shows a sanitized application demo workspace, not a live diagnosis. Scanner access depends on provider configuration and connectivity. Source: docs/PLANT_SCAN_ACCEPTANCE.md and docs/CHAT_PHOTO_ACCEPTANCE.md.

10 · Your AI Farm Buddy.

AI text chat answers farming questions. The text model does not inspect photos itself: photos use separate specialist tools, and supported results can be shared with chat. Voice input turns speech into editable words and never sends them automatically. Farmers can choose to share selected farm records with chat. The server selects the relevant records and reference material. This does not automatically train the AI, and chat cannot change farm records. Do not promise that outside providers retain no data. Spoken replies need separate setup and are not promised here. Source: docs/AI_FARM_CONTEXT.md, docs/WORKERS_AI_ACCEPTANCE.md and FarmAssistant.vue.

11 · Built for real farm work.

Offline workspace drafts are limited to event creation, task completion, task stock consumption and harvest creation. They are device/browser storage, not a full offline installation or background sync guarantee. Reconnect, review each draft and choose to send it. Errors or conflicting updates need review. The app checks the account, farm and permissions when sending. Online storage does not guarantee backups or uninterrupted access. Source: app/utils/offline-workspace.js, useFarmWorkspace.js and docs/CLOUDFLARE.md.

12 · Smart farming. One step at a time.

This is a practical invitation, not a free-trial, pricing or availability claim. Start by planning a season, assigning a task, recording a harvest or checking the cashbook. No invented user counts, revenue, TAM or accuracy figures appear in this deck.
