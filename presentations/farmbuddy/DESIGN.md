# Living clarity

FarmBuddy's presentation should feel like a well-kept farm: spacious, connected and immediately legible. Forest green anchors the story, warm yellow identifies the next action, and cream gives the eye room to rest. Every slide has one clear claim and one dominant visual.

Large rounded shapes echo the application without turning the deck into a collection of screenshots. A landscape introduces the product, a board expresses teamwork, and a single continuous path explains harvest, stock, sale and cashbook. Visuals do the explanatory work that paragraphs otherwise would.

Typography is bold, familiar and generous. Headings carry the point in a short sentence. Supporting words are labels, not essays. Small print is restricted to provenance and optional presenter notes, never an essential instruction.

Rhythm alternates between deep-green statements and quiet cream workspaces. Repeated spacing, corner radii and icon weights establish continuity, while composition changes with the feature being explained. Motion is short and optional, with reduced-motion support.

The craftsmanship is in restraint and accuracy. Illustrations are identified as illustrations. Product capabilities are grounded in the repository, and provider-dependent tools retain their actual limits. The deck does not invent customers, commercial traction, diagnostic accuracy, payment collection or satellite monitoring capabilities.
